What is EA SPORTS UFC 6?
Square up and fight your fight in the next evolution of the MMA simulation.
EA SPORTS UFC 6 is powered by fighters. Evolved striking and motion systems bring those UFC stars to life in the Octagon, while new game modes introduce immersive storytelling that makes every fight feel personal.
Markerless Capture delivers unprecedented fighter fidelity with authentic locomotion and Signature Strikes, complemented by new fighter bodies built with Sapien Technology that make your favorite UFC superstars truly look, move and fight like themselves.
Real-Time Contact brings the impact to the Octagon with Frostbite™-powered ragdoll physics and damage reactions. When you really start dishing out the damage, enter Flow State, which engages an athlete's standout skills in punishing fashion. All-new game modes like Hall of Legends and The Legacy let you experience the stories of UFC greats or carve your own path, from backrooms to the bright lights.
This game includes optional in-game purchases of virtual currency that can be used to acquire virtual in-game items.
Key features
Why you should play UFC 6
 Full-contact combat
With all-new Frostbite-powered ragdoll physics, contact windows, damage and hit reactions, the impact of any strike takes on a new, dangerous dimension. Real-Time Contact delivers more precise, fair and visceral exchanges, with each success feeling earned and every mistake costly.
 Big fight feeling
Thousands chanting a fighter’s name. The rush of energy when Bruce Buffer gets on the mic. The electricity of the crowd reacting to a knockout blow. UFC 6 delivers that big fight feeling with 3D spatial crowd audio and ambisonic sound for complete immersion, and dynamic crowd reactions that reflect authentic venues and fighters.
 Go with the Flow
This all-new feature introduces 30 unique fighter Flow States built around each athlete’s authentic strengths, tendencies and fight IQ. When you lean into a fighter’s style, you build momentum that unlocks an advantage. Flow State turns your fighter's identity into impact, forcing real-time adjustments as momentum shifts and the fight evolves.
What's new in UFC 6?
UFC 6 gameplay deep dive
Producer Raman Bassi provides an in-depth look at the new technology and gameplay systems that make UFC 6 the most true-to-life simulation of the combat sport to date.
UFC 6 game modes
Explore the Game Modes of your UFC 6 Journey.
 The Legacy
Step into the shoes of Chris Carter, a collegiate wrestling prospect rising through the regional scene with dreams of the Octagon. But his fight goes beyond the cage, grappling with his late father’s legacy and a fierce rivalry. Your choices shape Carter's journey in this cinematic, decision-driven prologue to Career Mode.
 Career Mode
Enter Career Mode with your Created Fighter, a UFC star, or continue Chris Carter’s journey from The Legacy. With an expanded decision-driven system, dual-title opportunities, a fight for the BMF title and a dynamic world that reacts to you, every choice shapes your path to greatness.
 Hall of Legends
Their performances in the Octagon cemented them in history; their upbringings forged them into fighters. Hall of Legends is an immersive experience where you explore the stories, environments and moments that shaped UFC superstars. Relive iconic fights through a seamless blend of footage, cinematics and gameplay.
