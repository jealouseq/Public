Licences below are as listed on the Modrinth API / project pages on the day of the search; confirm the licence file of the exact
version before bundling anything. "Depend on" (the player installs it separately) is always safer than "bundle" (we ship its files).

## Buildings
No ready-made building pack matches the real Bakurani layout. Generic schematic sites (MineSchematic, Spaleforce, McBuild,
PhantomMarket) give free files but no licence per build. kogtyv "Towny and Village" has a USSR panel-housing city type (1.21.x
datapack, licence not checked). Conclusion: keep generating buildings from the game's own map and hand-model the landmarks.

## In use
- **Gun models:** `research/tools/build_models.py guns` converts the CC0 "Ultimate Gun Pack" by Quaternius
  (https://opengameart.org/content/low-poly-guns-pack, CC0) into smooth-shaded OBJ meshes (`neoforge:obj` item models, one tinted
  material per part, a shared white 16x16 texture) for 33 guns; the source files stay in the git-ignored `local-data/mesh_cache`.
  Credit is not required for CC0 but is given here.
- **Wolf-110:** the "Soviet military off-road vehicle" from OpenGameArt (CC0), decimated per material and re-oriented.
- **All other vehicles, trees, buildings, decor:** original, generated by the scripts in `research/tools/` (no third-party assets).
- **Considered and not used:** Quaternius' Google Drive packs (download quota exceeded), Kenney and poly.pizza / Sketchfab (blocked from
  the build environment or licence unclear), minecraft-inside.ru maps (403 from the build environment; copying a map's blocks without a
  licence is not done, its post-Soviet street style was recreated by the generator instead), STALCRAFT and WARDOGS game files
  (proprietary: weapons, buildings and items in that style were drawn from scratch or from CC0 sources).

## Decor (furniture, street furniture, camp gear)
The mcpehub.org "furniture" category (https://mcpehub.org/mods/furniture/) lists Minecraft Bedrock (Pocket Edition) add-ons such as
MrCrayFish Furniture, Rustic Furniture, Modern Furniture and Gooblideco. They are Bedrock files (no Java or NeoForge build), their
listing states no licence or author terms, so none of their models or textures were taken. The mod has its own set instead:
`research/tools/gen_decor.py` draws 59 box-built blocks (chairs, tables, sofas, wardrobes, stoves, fridges, lamps, benches, mailboxes,
street lamps, sandbags, hesco barriers, crates, cots, workbenches, floodlights, barbed wire ...) with collision shapes that turn
with their facing, and writes the blockstates, item models and names. They are in the WARDOGS creative tab and are used by the world
generator for house interiors, yards, streets and outposts.
