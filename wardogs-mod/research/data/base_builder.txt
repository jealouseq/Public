WARDOGS Base Builder & FOB Planner
The WARDOGS Base Builder lets you plan a FOB before spending supplies in a match. Place walls, bunkers, towers and support structures from
59
buildable pieces, then inspect the layout from above or walk through it on foot.
The manifest counts the pieces in your plan and totals their supply cost. Use it to compare layouts, check access through a gate, and work out what your squad needs to bring for the next stage of construction.
Lay out the position
Choose an open pad or a mapped site, place your FOB and build outward. The editor shows the
60
m FOB build range. Socket snapping joins compatible pieces, while grid and free placement give you control over the rest of the layout. Inspect the terrain and placement warnings before treating a plan as ready to build.
Budget each build stage
Start with the pieces your squad can afford to place first. Add stages for later fortifications, check the manifest as the plan changes, and compare the supply total before adding another tower or wall. Costs follow the buildable data shipped with the site; a rebalance can change an older plan's total.
Reuse and share a layout
Community bases open directly in the editor so you can study a layout and adapt it. Prefabs keep a group of pieces together for repeated placement. Sign in to save a plan to the hub, keep it private with a share link, or publish it for other players. The squad controls let you work on a live plan together.
Questions people ask
Do I need an account to start building?
No. You can use the editor without signing in. Sign in when you want to save a base to the hub and manage its sharing.
Can I inspect a base from ground level?
Yes. Switch from the overview to the on-foot view to check routes, entrances and sightlines inside the layout.
Does opening a community base change the original?
No. Opening another player's plan gives you a starting point in the builder. Saving your version creates your own base.
