Reference
WARDOGS Progression
WARDOGS gates gear behind two kinds of level. The Wardog level (the career) is the sum of all six class levels and drives account-wide unlocks: armor tiers, backpacks, sidearms and ammunition types. Each class ladder (Infantry, Medic, Recon, Support, Driver, Pilot) unlocks the gear of its trade. Reaching a level is not the whole gate: every unlock also asks a one-time cash payment before the vendor sells it.
Paying every gate on every ladder costs $12,736,500 on top of the levels themselves. Every item links to its database entry, and the same level and price sit on the entry itself.
Reading the board
Set each class card to your in-game level and the board does the rest: the career banner sums them, open levels light up, and the next gate on each ladder is marked. Levels are remembered on this device only.
A price covers its whole level row, so two items on one row share a single payment.

Questions people ask
How does progression work in WARDOGS?
Two ladders run at once. The Wardog career level is the sum of your six class levels and unlocks account-wide gear such as armor, backpacks and sidearms. Each class (Infantry, Medic, Recon, Support, Driver, Pilot) levels separately through play and unlocks its own weapons and tools. Every unlock then asks a one-time cash payment.
What is the Wardog level?
The career level: the sum of all six class levels. Class XP comes from playing the class, so the Wardog level rises with everything you do.
What unlocks at Wardog level 150?
Arsenal Backpack + 2 Slings, for $175,000. It is the last gate on the career ladder.
How much does it cost to unlock everything in WARDOGS?
$12,736,500 across 264 unlocks, paid one gate at a time. The class ladders carry most of the weapons; the career ladder carries armor, backpacks and ammunition types.
